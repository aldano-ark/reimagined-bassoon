Track owned cards and a wishlist in two independent example apps. Seven bundled cards, saved locally, with the starter apps kept intact.

A real iOS walkthrough: find a card, save it to your wishlist, and add copies to your collection. Silent video with optional step captions.

Read the walkthrough
  1. Open the card catalog from the empty collection and search for Pikachu by its printed number, 25.
  2. Open Pikachu’s details and save the card to the wishlist.
  3. Add two copies, choose Reverse holo and Lightly played, and enter a note.
  4. Save the entry. The collection shows one card and two copies, retained after reopening the app.
